# CleanAI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

CleanAI is an iPhone app that removes hidden data (metadata) from photos and videos you choose.

## 1. What we collect

Nothing. CleanAI has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## 2. Your photos and videos

Files you pick are processed entirely on your iPhone. They are never uploaded, and we never see them. Temporary working copies are deleted when you start over or reopen the app. CleanAI only adds to your photo library when you tap Save to Photos; it cannot read your library beyond the items you select in the system picker.

## 3. Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

## 4. Children

CleanAI does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## 5. What CleanAI does and does not do

CleanAI removes data stored inside your files. It does not change how your photo looks, and it cannot control labels that a platform applies based on its own analysis of an image.

## 6. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
