## Common questions

**What does CleanAI remove?**
Hidden data stored inside photos and videos: AI tags, Content Credentials (C2PA), GPS location, device and camera details, editing software and history, dates, author and copyright fields.

**Does it change how my photo looks?**
No. The picture keeps its resolution. Only the hidden data is removed, and your original file is never modified; CleanAI saves a clean copy.

**A platform still labelled my post. Why?**
CleanAI removes data stored in the file. Platforms can also apply labels based on their own analysis of the image, which no metadata tool can control.

**Are my files uploaded anywhere?**
No. Everything happens on your iPhone.

**How do I cancel CleanAI Pro?**
Open the App Store, tap your profile picture, then Subscriptions, choose CleanAI Pro and tap Cancel. You can also open Settings in CleanAI and tap Manage Subscription.

**How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone?**
Open Settings in CleanAI and tap Restore Purchases.
